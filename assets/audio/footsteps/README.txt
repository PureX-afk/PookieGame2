sand-step-01.wav
================

Cut from the user's own recording:
  Chrome Downloads/Minecraft_Walking_on_Sand_Sound_Effect.mp3

It is one step lifted from the walking sequence at 6.325-6.545 s of the source.
The rest of the recording is other steps and is not used.

Processing:
  - mixed to mono (the game pans each step itself)
  - DC offset removed
  - 4 ms fade-in and 60 ms squared fade-out
  - normalized to a -9.1 dBFS peak


grass-step-01.wav
=================

Cut from the user's own recording:
  Chrome Downloads/Minecraft_Walking_-_Sound_Effect__ProSounds.mp3

The recording is one walk of eleven footfalls, about 0.39 s apart in 4.6 s. The third
of them is kept, at 1.056-1.356 s of the source; the rest is not used. It was chosen
because it has the quietest lead-in and stops 70 ms before the next footfall, so one
slice is exactly one step.

Processing:
  - mixed to mono (the game pans each step itself)
  - 4 ms fade-in and 60 ms squared fade-out
  - normalized to a -9.1 dBFS peak
