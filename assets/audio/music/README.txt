Beginning.mp3
=============

Background music supplied by the user (Chrome Downloads/Beginning.mp3).

- 1:42, stereo MP3
- First song in the playlist.
- Starts when a session starts and stops when returning to the launch screen.
- Its level lives in Settings > Volume > Music and is saved with the game.

Haggstrom.mp3
=============

Background music supplied by the user
(Chrome Downloads/C418_-_Haggstrom_-_Minecraft_Volume_Alpha.mp3).

- 3:24, stereo MP3 ("Haggstrom", C418, Minecraft Volume Alpha)
- Plays after Beginning; the two alternate for the whole session.
- Settings > Volume has a Song row that switches between them and a Shuffle row
  that picks each next song at random (never the same one twice in a row).
- The selected song and the shuffle switch are saved with the game, along with
  the music level.
